# Named lane dispatch

**Owner** role dispatcher. **Consumers** operator and accountable lead.

Launch a named operational lead or implementer, or an expressly requested
audit lane, through `spawn-agent` in its own named, interactive tmux window
with the requested model and the appropriate worktree. Verify the live pane
identity, model and worktree, and record task delivery from new transport
evidence. Report a blocked launch or an unconfirmed brief accurately.
Inspect any existing child and preserve the no-replay boundary before
considering another action. The lane stays independently visible and
steerable. A native subagent or one-shot headless call cannot fulfill this
dispatch.

The explicit native same-provider council seat retains its council route.
An explicitly requested internal helper can use the host's native tool for
its bounded task. Helper results and transport receipts carry their own
meaning. Boundaries are exercised at dispatch with a named lead,
implementer and audit lane, a council seat, a scoped helper, and a failed
or ambiguous spawn.
