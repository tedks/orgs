# Named lane dispatch

**Owner** role dispatcher. **Consumers** operator and accountable lead.

Launch a named operational lead or implementer, or an expressly requested
audit lane, through `spawn-agent` in its own named, interactive tmux window
with the requested model and the worktree assigned by the dispatcher for
the package. Launch without a task, verify the live pane identity, provider,
model and worktree, then brief the child through `tmux-message`. If identity
cannot be verified or differs from the assignment, withhold the brief,
preserve the observed child and escalate
to the accountable lead. A fresh transport receipt confirms task delivery.
Report a blocked launch or an unconfirmed brief accurately.
Inspect any existing child and preserve the no-replay boundary before
considering another action. The lane stays independently visible and
steerable. A native subagent or one-shot headless call cannot fulfill this
dispatch.

The explicit native same-provider council seat retains its council route.
The assigning lead or an operator can explicitly request a scoped internal
helper for a bounded task. The helper cannot take ownership of a named lane,
lead role, implementation package, or requested audit lane. Tier alone
does not select a dispatch route. The helper's result attests to its own
task. A lane's transport receipt establishes delivery only. Boundary checks
exercise dispatch with a named lead, implementer and audit lane, a council
seat, a scoped helper, and a failed or ambiguous spawn.
